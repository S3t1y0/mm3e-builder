Mutants & Masterminds 3e Character Builder & Tactical Sheet

A digital character builder, tactical character sheet, and power construction workspace for the Mutants & Masterminds 3rd Edition (M&M 3e / D20 Hero System) tabletop roleplaying game.

Key Features

  • Tactical character sheet with dynamic defense, ability, and combat calculations
  • Guided Character Creation Wizard with pre-configured archetypes
  • Power Studio: Custom superpower builder with alternate effect arrays, flaws, and extras
  • Equipment and vehicle budgeting
  • Roll20 Virtual Tabletop (VTT) companion integration
