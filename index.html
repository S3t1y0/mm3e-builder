Automated Power Point Bookkeeping & PL Validation

Building superhero characters in the D20 Hero System requires careful mathematical budgeting. This application automates all Power Point (PP) bookkeeping, ability modifiers, and Power Level (PL) cap calculations.

The builder dynamically verifies offensive caps (Attack Bonus + Effect Rank) and defensive caps (Dodge/Parry + Toughness, Fortitude + Will) in accordance with official Mutants & Masterminds 3rd Edition rules.

Core Features for Superhero Tabletop RPG Campaigns

  • Tactical Character Sheet: Manage active conditions, dynamic defenses, attack maneuvers, initiative rolls, and hero point reserves during live play.
  • Creation Wizard: Build heroes from the ground up or select pre-configured archetypes such as Battlesuit, Speedster, Mystic, Paragon, and Martial Artist.
  • Superpower Studio: Design custom superpowers with dynamic extras, flat modifiers, flaws, and alternate effect arrays with automated cost-per-rank calculations.
  • Official Sheet Print (PDF): Export clean, authentic two-page Green Ronin style character sheets optimized for A4 and Letter print formats.
  • Roll20 VTT Integration: Generate one-click Roll20 chat macros, markdown summaries, BBCode forum sheets, and connect with our companion browser extension.

Step-by-Step Character Creation Guide

First, choose your campaign Power Level. Standard superhero games start at Power Level 10 with a starting budget of 150 Power Points.

Next, allocate your primary ability scores across Strength, Agility, Fighting, Awareness, Stamina, Dexterity, Intellect, and Presence.

Then, craft your superpowers using base effect types like Damage, Affliction, Movement, and Protection. Apply modifiers to fit your superhero origin story.

Finally, balance your defensive caps and select essential combat advantages and skills to complete your hero profile.
