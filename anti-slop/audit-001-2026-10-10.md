## Numbered Findings List

### [01] R-25 — Color Contrast Failure on Printed/White Cards
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location:** `src/styles/green-ronin-sheet.css:68`, `src/styles/green-ronin-sheet.css:74`, `src/styles/green-ronin-sheet.css:285`, `src/styles/green-ronin-sheet.css:388`
- **Pattern:** `.gr-card-title`, `.gr-empty-hint`, `.gr-defense-title-bar`, and `.gr-offense-title` use `color: #94a3b8` (Slate 400).
- **Reason:** `#94a3b8` on a pure white background (`#ffffff`) has a contrast ratio of only ~2.7:1, violating WCAG AA minimum contrast of 4.5:1 for body and normal label text.
- **Remediation:** Darken these labels on the character sheet to `#64748b` (Slate 500, ~4.6:1) or `#475569` (Slate 600, ~5.9:1) to ensure high print and screen readability.

### [02] R-32 — Keyboard Accessibility on Identity Radio Toggles
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location:** `src/components/modals/GreenRoninSheet.vue:51-60`
- **Pattern:** `SECRET` / `PUBLIC` identity toggle uses bare `<span>` tags with `@click` only.
- **Reason:** The options cannot be focused or toggled using keyboard navigation (`Tab`, `Space`, `Enter`).
- **Remediation:** Add `role="radio"`, `:aria-checked="heroStore.character.isSecretIdentity"`, `tabindex="0"`, and `@keydown.space.prevent` / `@keydown.enter.prevent` handlers.

### [03] R-32 — Keyboard Accessibility on Combat Condition Cards
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location:** `src/components/modals/ConditionPickerModal.vue:88-98`
- **Pattern:** `.cond-select-card` wrappers are clickable `<div>` elements without `tabindex` or keyboard listeners.
- **Reason:** Users navigating via keyboard can access search and tabs, but cannot focus or activate individual condition cards.
- **Remediation:** Add `tabindex="0"`, `role="button"`, and `@keydown.enter.prevent` / `@keydown.space.prevent` to make every condition card selectable via keyboard.

### [04] R-03 — Mobile Viewport Overflow & Sub-44px Tap Targets
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location:** `src/components/modals/GreenRoninPrintModal.vue:105-114` & `src/components/sheet/AbilitiesMatrix.vue:182`
- **Pattern:** 
  - (A) `.gr-preview-scroll` wraps a fixed 210mm paper sheet without explicit mobile horizontal scroll containment.
  - (B) `.matrix-absent-toggle` button has a rendered height of ~16px with no touch hit-box expansion.
- **Reason:** On mobile viewports (< 768px), the 210mm print preview causes viewport clipping; touch targets under 44px fail mobile ergonomics.
- **Remediation:** Add `overflow-x: auto; max-width: 100%; -webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch;` to `.gr-preview-scroll`; add a `::before` touch expander to `.matrix-absent-toggle` to guarantee a 44x44px touch area.

### [05] R-04 — Universal Fallback Lightning Bolt Icon
- **Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Location:** `src/rules/powerEngine.js:66-67`
- **Pattern:** `BASE_EFFECT_ICONS[baseEffect] || 'ri-flashlight-line'` falls back to a generic lightning bolt for unknown power effects.
- **Reason:** Universal lightning bolts on all abilities is a classic AI default fallback without contextual relevance.
- **Remediation:** Provide categorized fallback icons based on power action and descriptor (e.g. `ri-shield-line` for defense/resist, `ri-crosshair-2-line` for targeted attacks, `ri-magic-line` for exotic effects).

### [06] antislop-code — Decorative Banner Separators in Code Comments
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Locks)
- **Location:** `src/components/modals/GreenRoninSheet.vue:3, 400` & `src/styles/green-ronin-sheet.css:80, 209, 435, 755`
- **Pattern:** Repeated character banner comments (`<!-- ================= PAGE 1 ================= -->`, `/* ==================== */`) and step numbering (`<!-- 1. TOP HEADER BANNER -->`).
- **Reason:** Decorative ASCII banners and step narration are telltale signatures of AI coding generators that add clutter without carrying semantic value.
- **Remediation:** Clean up all banner blocks to standard single-line comments (`<!-- Page 1: Core Official Sheet -->`, `/* Header Section */`).

### [07] antislop-copywriting — Formulaic Persona Prompt Placeholder
- **Priority:** LOW (Quality Locks)
- **Location:** `src/components/modals/GreenRoninSheet.vue:469`
- **Pattern:** `placeholder="Enter hero background story, history, motivation, and tactics here..."`
- **Reason:** Narrative listing ("story, history, motivation, and tactics here...") reads like an AI prompt template.
- **Remediation:** Replace with authentic tabletop RPG character dossier phrasing: `placeholder="Hero background, origin story, motivations, and tactical combat profile..."`.
